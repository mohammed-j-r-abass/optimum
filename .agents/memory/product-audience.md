---
name: FirstStep audience and scope
description: User-stated audience and principles for career preparation.
---
FirstStep is primarily for graduating, first-time job seekers from rural areas who have not had access to CV-writing and interview preparation support. The user wants an appealing MVP, not something overly fancy.

**Why:** The user described a final-year student repeatedly applying without knowing what employers expect.

**How to apply:** Use beginner-friendly language; help users identify real evidence from volunteering, school and everyday responsibilities. Discover relevant experience through questions, explain skill gaps constructively, and let the user proceed with an honest application even when the fit is weak. Do not assume a rural location itself implies limited skills.

Live audio-and-visual conversation is core MVP scope, not an optional later enhancement. The user explicitly rejected typed chat or recorded turn-by-turn voice with manually shared snapshots as a substitute.

**Why:** The user identifies a live AI interviewer that can hear and see the applicant as the product's main distinction from ordinary interview advice and text chat.

**How to apply:** Prioritize a supported real-time multimodal provider and an actual live conversation. Clearly disclose provider connection requirements rather than silently reducing scope. Visual coaching must stay grounded in observable presentation rather than inferred emotions, personality or employability.

The AI must lead novice applicants through recommended actions and ask for missing facts, not ask "Do you want me to do this?" or make them choose resume conventions. The user repeated that applicants do not know what employers expect. Use full known institution names automatically; clarify genuinely ambiguous facts without asking stylistic preferences.

**Why:** The user tested the app and found permission-seeking and expert-choice questions unhelpful despite the features working.

**How to apply:** Apply this across text, voice, interview preparation and feedback. Explain what will improve the application and ask for evidence. Preserve factual accuracy and explicit user corrections. CV coaching is discovery-only: no resume drafts, sections or polished bullets in chat. Write the actual CV only when the user clicks the generation button, selecting relevant facts from the whole conversation.

The coach must clearly advise against including experiences that do not match the target job and explain why. For a relevant skill not yet evidenced, explicitly ask whether the user has ever performed a concrete related task and invite them to narrate an example. Broader role knowledge must be labelled separately from requirements stated in the advert.

**Why:** The user asked for information gathering rather than chat-generated resumes, explicit relevance decisions, and evidence questions about missing skills.

**How to apply:** Do not dismiss unpaid/community experience when the activities demonstrate a requested transferable skill; do not invent a match for unrelated activities. Apply relevance advice during CV generation and never turn a suggested skill into a claimed ability without user evidence.

Before collecting CV facts, explain what the specific employer's job advert asks for, the required versus optional skills, and the evidence the applicant needs.

**Why:** The user said the coach did not explain what Lumen wanted or the skills they would need.

**How to apply:** Ground the explanation in the supplied advert; label unclear requirements and do not invent company-specific preferences from a company name.

The user wants resumes matching their supplied compact reference: maximise one page, 12-point Times New Roman body text, 0.5-inch margins on every side, close line spacing, bold centered name, aligned entries and bold section headings separated by rules. Conversations should feel like WhatsApp with distinct colours and green AI messages.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected the plain-text resume design and unclear conversational presentation.

**How to apply:** Preserve these visual choices in preview and actual downloadable PDF, without importing app navigation into the document. Condense relevant writing rather than shrinking below 12 points or clipping content. Do not claim Times-Roman is exact Times New Roman; actual font files are required to embed the latter.

The user wants chat and CV sections independently expandable to full-screen focus and restorable, and the left navigation/account panel openable and closeable to reclaim space.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to focus on each workspace section without the other panels taking space.

**How to apply:** Preserve unsent text, pending replies and media state when changing layout; keep a visible restore/reopen control.
